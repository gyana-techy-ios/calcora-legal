1About this policy

Pixel Astro makes simple, offline utility apps such as calculators, converters and everyday tools. Calcora, our calculator app, is one example. This single policy covers every Pixel Astro app you download from Google Play or the Apple App Store.

The short version: all features run on your device. We have no backend server, no account system, no login, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own, and our apps do not collect your personal data. The only third-party SDK is Google AdMob, which shows the ads that pay for the apps.

3Data stored on your device

Anything you create in the app, such as your history, favorites and settings, is kept in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

  • You can remove it by clearing it inside the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your system settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it as well.
  • Your phone's own backup (Google backup on Android, iCloud on iPhone) may include app data. That is controlled by your device settings and the backup provider, not by us.

4Ads and Google AdMob

To keep the apps free, we show ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). This is the only third-party SDK in our apps, and ads are our only source of revenue. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may process:

  • Advertising identifier. On Android, the Android Advertising ID. On iOS, the IDFA, but only if you allow tracking when the app asks (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, or the app does not ask, the IDFA is not accessed.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location).
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads shown, tapped or closed.
  • Device and diagnostic information, such as OS version, device model and basic performance or crash details of the ad SDK.

Google uses this to serve ads, measure how they perform, and prevent fraud and abuse. Google acts under its own privacy practices, so please read:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob and Firebase data disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal data. We also do not use data for advertising profiling, data brokerage or any other revenue activity. The only revenue is the ads shown in the app.

5Your ad choices

Consent in Europe and the UK

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a Google consent form (Google UMP) the first time. You can choose personalized or non-personalized ads. You can change your mind later using the privacy or ad choices option in the app's Settings, where available.

Android

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices, Delete advertising ID).
  • You can also opt out of ad personalization on the same screen.

iPhone and iPad

  • Limit tracking: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track", or switch tracking off for a single app.

Declining personalized ads does not remove ads. They are simply less tailored to you.

6Permissions

Our apps ask for only two things: Internet and network state, so ads can load. We do not use your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or files.

If a specific app has a feature that clearly needs one of these (for example, choosing a photo), it will ask you at that moment. The permission is used only on your device for that feature, and what it touches is never uploaded. You can deny or revoke it at any time in your system settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

7Languages

Our apps are offered in several languages. The language you pick is saved only on your device and changes nothing about how data is handled. This policy applies equally in every language the apps are offered in. If a translated version ever differs from this English version, the English version prevails.

8Security

  • Your data stays in your device's sandboxed app storage, which other apps cannot normally read.
  • We do not transmit anything you create or enter.
  • Ad traffic goes over HTTPS/TLS, handled by Google's SDK.

No system is perfectly secure, but because we hold no data about you, there is very little to protect or lose on our side. Keep your device updated and locked for the best protection.

9Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, including to access, correct, delete, or object to its use.

  • GDPR / UK GDPR (EEA and UK): access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawing consent at any time.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined.
  • India DPDP Act 2023: to access, correct and erase your data, and to seek grievance redress.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For requests about ad data, contact Google through its privacy tools linked in section 4. We are still happy to answer any question: email gp.ios91@gmail.com.

10Children

Our apps are general-audience utilities and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. Ad requests can be handled by AdMob in the way appropriate for the audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information, please contact us.
