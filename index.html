Privacy Policy — Calcora

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Applies to the Calcora Android and iOS apps.

Calcora is a calculator app (EMI, SIP, GST, scientific, unit conversion, engineering, and date calculations). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

Calcora does all of its calculations on your device, offline. We do not operate any server, we do not collect the numbers you type in, and we do not require an account. The only data handling in this app comes from the advertising shown to keep it free — described below.

What Calcora itself does with your data

DataCollected by us?Where it lives
Numbers you type into any calculatorNoOnly in the app's memory while you're using it
History & Favorites (calculations you chose to save)No — stays on your devicePrivate app storage, included in your device's own backup if you use one

Name, email, phone, contacts, location, camera, filesNever requested—

The app requests only the Internet and network-state permissions, needed solely so the advertising SDK can function. We do not integrate any analytics or crash-reporting tool of our own.

Advertising

Calcora is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, Google's SDK may process, on your device and on Google's servers:

  • A device advertising identifier (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • App interaction data (e.g. which ads were shown or tapped)

This happens subject to the consent choices you make where the law requires them (for example, the EU/UK/Switzerland consent form shown on first launch there), and to your own device's ad settings. You can review and change your consent choice at any time from Settings > Privacy & Data > Ad privacy choices inside the app. On iPhone, Calcora does not request permission to track you, and does not read your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

The numbers you enter into a calculator are never sent to AdMob, to us, or to any other party.

For more detail on how Google's advertising products handle data, see Google's own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and AdMob's data disclosure.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share any data with third parties ourselves — the only data leaving your device is what Google's advertising SDK itself collects, as described above, under Google's own privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

History and Favorites are stored only on your device and only until you delete them. You can clear all saved history at any time from Settings > Privacy & Data > Delete all saved history, or by uninstalling the app. Because we never receive or store this data on any server of ours, there is nothing for us to delete on our end — uninstalling the app removes everything.

Children's privacy

Calcora is a general-purpose calculator utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
